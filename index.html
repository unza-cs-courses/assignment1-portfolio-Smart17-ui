Ka Jowa App

Ka job is a mobile/web application designed to connect people who need small tasks done with individuals looking for quick, flexible job opportunities. The app allows users to post short-term or small jobs such as cleaning, deliveries, tutoring,repairs, or event assistance, and enables job seekers to find and apply for these opportunities easily. In many communities(especially among students and unemployed youth), people struggle to find short-term or part-time work. At the same time, many individuals need small tasks done but don't know where to find reliable help

Emergency app

The Emergency Response App is a web/mobile application designed to help users quickly request assistance during emergency situations.
