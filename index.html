Ka Jowa is a mobile and web application designed to connect people who need small tasks done with individuals looking for flexible short-term jobs. Tasks may include cleaning, deliveries, tutoring, repairs, and event assistance. The platform helps communities find help easily while providing job opportunities for students and unemployed youth.
